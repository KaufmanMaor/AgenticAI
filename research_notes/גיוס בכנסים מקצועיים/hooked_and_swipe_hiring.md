# Hooked (Israel) and the history of "Tinder for jobs" swipe-based hiring apps

Research date: 2026-10-13. Method note: direct page fetching was blocked in this environment (DNS/proxy 403), so findings below come from search-engine result snippets/summaries of the cited pages, not full-text reads. Treat precise numbers as "as reported in snippet" and verify against the original page before publishing.

## Q1. What exactly is Hooked (Israel), who is Barzani, how does it work, is it event/location-based, any traction?

### Takeaway
Hooked ("The Hooked App") is an Israeli **event-based dating app**, not a hiring app. It was founded by **Noa Barazani** (Founder & CEO; often misspelled "Barzani") and **Roi Revach**, who started it as students at Reichman University. Organizers post a QR code at a venue. Attendees scan it and see which other singles are at the same event. Traction is small but real: about 70+ events in 6 Israeli cities (reported Feb 2026), roughly 15,000 downloads (reported Sept 2026), a viral moment around bomb shelters during the 2026 Iran war, and a US "Hooked Day" with the NFL's Arizona Cardinals (Sept 2026). I found no reported funding.

### Cited Findings
- Noa Barazani's LinkedIn lists her as "Founder and CEO at The Hooked App" — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/noa-barazani/)
- Co-founded by Noa Barazani and Roi Revach. They first built it while studying at Reichman University (Herzliya), "initially targeting parties, conferences and large events" — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/culture/article-888649); [Yahoo syndication](https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/articles/single-bomb-shelter-israeli-app-140927054.html)
- How it works:
  - Event organizers get a QR code, which they post at the venue entrance and online.
  - Singles create a profile and either browse a list of events to register in advance or scan the QR code when they arrive.
  - They then see a list of singles at that event who match what they're looking for, and others at the event can see their profile.
  
  Source: [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/culture/article-888649); [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/society/artc-bomb-shelter-dating-israeli-app-turns-safe-rooms-into-matchmaking-hubs)
- Traction (Israel): Barazani said the app had been used at **more than 70 events across six cities in Israel**, per a Jerusalem Post piece reported as February 2026 — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/culture/article-888649)
- Wartime pivot: after "Operation Roaring Lion" (the 2026 Israel–Iran war) began, the founders adapted the app so people could set up an "event" in their bomb shelter and post the QR code at the shelter entrance. Barazani's reasoning: wartime rules capped public gatherings but not designated shelters — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/culture/article-888649); [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/society/artc-bomb-shelter-dating-israeli-app-turns-safe-rooms-into-matchmaking-hubs); [Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/world/israel-iran-war/bomb-shelter-dating-qr-codes-hooked-app-survival-tech-3026043.html)
- The idea spread after a viral X post by Yael Bar Tur, which US Ambassador Mike Huckabee reposted — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/culture/article-888649)
- Hooked was part of a March 2026 roundup of Israeli wartime apps ("Shower, shelter, swipe") — [J. Weekly, 2026-03-05](https://jweekly.com/2026/03/05/shower-shelter-swipe-israels-startup-nation-meets-iran-war-with-a-wave-of-apps/); [Goodnet](https://goodnet.org/articles/love-finding-way-even-in-shelters)
- US expansion: the Arizona Cardinals' home opener against the Seattle Seahawks at State Farm Stadium (Sept 20, per the FOS newsletter date of 2026-09-20) was branded **"Hooked Day."**
  - Fans who bought tickets through Hooked's event link were prompted 24 hours before kickoff to install the app and build a profile.
  - QR codes were placed around the stadium for everyone else.
  - Barazani said close to 200 tickets were sold through the link.
  - The deal started when a viral Instagram video in May caught the eye of a Cardinals staffer.
  
  Source: [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/sports-teams-are-playing-a-new-role-matchmaker/); [FOS newsletter](https://frontofficesports.com/newsletter/why-teams-are-playing-matchmaker/); [Fox10 Phoenix](https://www.fox10phoenix.com/video/fmc-qbbke7t10zu2hyb9)
- FOS described Hooked as "less than six months old with roughly 15,000 downloads," still in start-up mode, and wanting to expand to other stadiums. All app activity disappears after an event ends (ephemeral by design) — [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/sports-teams-are-playing-a-new-role-matchmaker/)
- The iOS listing is "The Hooked App," and the website (hooked-app.com) has an FAQ — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-hooked-app/id6748921014); [hooked-app.com FAQ](https://hooked-app.com/faq)

### Inferences
- Hooked's mechanic is directly relevant to recruiting at professional conferences:
  - presence-gated discovery: you only see people physically at the same event
  - access by QR code at the door
  - a pool that is pre-filtered by intent ("singles here")
  - ephemerality: data vanishes after the event
  
  The founders' original target already included "conferences." Swapping "singles" for "candidates/hiring managers at this conference" is an obvious adaptation. I found no evidence Hooked has done this.
- The "less than six months old" description (Sept 2026) conflicts with "70+ events" reported in Feb 2026. Possible explanations: FOS meant the US app or relaunch (the App Store ID 6748921014 suggests a mid-2025 listing), or FOS was simply inaccurate. Treat the app's age as uncertain. The Israeli product appears to have been live since at least 2025.
- Event-scoped matching solves the cold-start problem that kills general swipe apps: density comes from the event, not from the app's own user base.

### Gaps
- Funding: I found no reported funding round, investors or valuation (searched English sources; Hebrew searches returned nothing).
- No Hebrew-press coverage (Geektime/Calcalist/Globes) surfaced. There was no outcome data from the Cardinals event (matches, conversion) and no monetization model (ticket revenue share? organizer fees?).
- "Barzani" in the brief appears to be Noa **Barazani**. There is no evidence of a link to other people named Barzani.

## Q2. Which swipe-to-hire apps existed, what were their outcomes, and why did most fail?

### Takeaway
The "Tinder for jobs" wave peaked in 2014–2017. Most of these apps were shut down, folded into an acquirer, or abandoned:
- Jobr was bought by Monster and later discontinued as a standalone app.
- Switch went dark.
- Shapr closed in 2023.
- Bumble Bizz was phased out (2025–26).

The survivors moved away from pure swiping: Hired's curated marketplace, which ended up folded into Adecco/LHH; Job Today's hourly/service-sector focus; Lunchclub's AI-curated 1:1 intros. Documented reasons for failure:
- thin, non-fungible talent pools
- low added value over one-click applying
- recruiters not engaging or not posting, which forced listings to be scraped from aggregators
- dependence on LinkedIn's API
- swiping doesn't change how recruiters actually process candidates

### Cited Findings: catalog
**Jobr (US, San Francisco)**
- Raised a $2M seed in July 2014 and hired Coatue's TJ Nahigian as CEO — [TechCrunch 2014-07-17](https://techcrunch.com/2014/07/17/jobr-2m)
- Monster acquired it in June 2016 (terms undisclosed). TechCrunch reported at the time that the app would be integrated into Monster, not shut down — [TechCrunch 2016-06-08](https://techcrunch.com/2016/06/08/monster-snaps-up-tinder-for-jobs-app-jobr); [Tech.co](https://tech.co/news/monster-acquires-mobile-job-search-startup-jobr-2016-06)
- Later sources: the standalone app was shut down, its features went into Monster's app, and it no longer has a website or iOS app. A price of about $12–12.5M comes only from an informal podcast remark (unverified) — [Jobtrain blog](https://jobtrain.co.uk/blog/switch-and-jobr-tinder-for-jobs)
- It relied on LinkedIn sign-in until LinkedIn locked down its API, then had to move to Facebook login and résumé upload. Few recruiters posted, so it pulled listings from Indeed's API — [Jobtrain blog](https://jobtrain.co.uk/blog/switch-and-jobr-tinder-for-jobs)

**Switch (US, NYC; founder Yarden Tadmor, an Israeli-born founder, though the company was US-based)**
- Founded January 2014. It was self-funded for about 10 months, then raised a $1.4M seed in December 2014 (Metamorphic Ventures, SG VC, BAM, Rhodium) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switch_(app)); [BestTechie](https://www.besttechie.com/switch-tinder-for-jobs-raises-1-4m/)
- Closed its seed at $2M in May 2015 — [TechCrunch 2015-05-22](https://techcrunch.com/2015/05/22/switch-with-2-million-in-seed-funding-is-tinder-for-jobs)
- Mechanics:
  - daily job recommendations; swipe to apply
  - the candidate stays anonymous until a mutual match, then chat opens
  - started with NYC tech jobs, then expanded to other US metros and to retail, healthcare and education
  - reported more than 400k applications by May 2015
  - iOS-first
  
  Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switch_(app)); [CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/247193/find-your-next-job-with-the-tinder-like-switch-app.html)
- Outcome: one blog says it kept growing until about July 2021, when growth suddenly stopped. No working site or app listing can be found today. No shutdown announcement or reason was found — [Jobtrain blog](https://jobtrain.co.uk/blog/switch-and-jobr-tinder-for-jobs)

**Shapr (Paris → NYC; professional networking by swipe)**
- Founded 2015. It raised a $9.5M Series A in June 2017. Reported in 2017: about 500k users, growing roughly 2,000 a day — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapr); [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/shapr-leve-95-millions-de-dollars-pour-lancer-une-version-payante-de-son-application-de-networking/297334); [Xconomy ($3.1M earlier round)](https://legacy.xconomy.com/?p=290697)
- Mechanics: swiping with mutual opt-in, a capped number of daily swipes, and a network cap of 50 — [Fintechnews HK](https://fintechnews.hk/1072/various/shapr-matchmaking-app-a-tinder-for-entrepreneurs/)
- Outcome: it closed in 2023 after being acquired by "Lincoln Group" (Wikipedia/HandWiki) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapr); [HandWiki](https://handwiki.org/wiki/Organization:Shapr). A blog instead claims it went inactive in early 2026 — [scale.jobs](https://scale.jobs/blog/linkedin-vs-lunchclub-vs-shapr-best-professional-networking-apps). The sources conflict; Wikipedia's 2023 date is more likely.

**Bumble Bizz (Bumble's networking mode)**
- Bizz was one of Bumble's three modes (Date/BFF/Bizz). Bumble support now says Bizz is no longer available: profiles are hidden, Bizz Mode can't be activated, and the company is focusing on dating and friends. The page was updated around Sept 2026, and one Bumble page says it is being phased out globally — [Bumble support](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/30208230972189-Availability-of-Bumble-Bizz); [Bumble help](https://bumble.com/en/bff/help/bumble-bizz)
- Related: Bumble For Friends launched as a standalone app in July 2023 and was replaced in the US by a new BFF app in September 2025 — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230726935186/en/Bumble-Inc.-Introduces-Bumble-For-Friends-Friendship-finding-App); [Fast Company SA](https://fastcompany.co.za/fast-company/business/2025-09-19-bumbles-new-bff-app-aims-to-spark-the-great-friennassance)

**Hired (US; reverse marketplace where companies bid on candidates, not strictly swipe-based)**
- In October 2020, The Information reported that Hired was selling assets and winding down after its CEO Mehul Patel abruptly resigned. In November 2020, Vettery (owned by Adecco, bought for $100M in 2018) acquired Hired and merged the two under the Hired brand — [TechCrunch 2020-11-23](https://techcrunch.com/2020/11/23/vettery-acquires-hired/amp/); [Recruiting News Network](https://www.recruitingnewsnetwork.com/posts/youre-not-hired-vettery-acquires-a-deeply-troubled-hired); [Wikipedia: Adecco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adecco)
- As of June 2024 it had been integrated into Adecco's LHH Recruitment Solutions — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/hired); [LongBoard newsletter](https://longboard-hcmacquisitions.beehiiv.com/p/hcm-acquisition-news-hiredcom-update-learn-favorite-influencer-will-help-find-job)

**Job Today (London/Spain; mobile hiring for hourly and service-sector jobs, often called "Tinder for recruiters")**
- Launched in Spain in 2015. Raised a $10M Series A in January 2016 (Accel lead, with Felix and Mangrove), when it reported 10k candidates hired in 8 months. Raised a $20M Series B in November 2016 (Flint Capital lead) — [TechCrunch 2016-01-27](https://techcrunch.com/2016/01/27/accel-leads-10m-series-a-for-jobtodays-service-industry-hiring-app); [TechCrunch 2016-11-10](https://techcrunch.com/2016/11/10/job-today-bags-20m-to-grow-its-service-industry-hiring-app-in-europe-and-beyond/?ncid=rss)
- Late 2016: 2M job seekers registered in the previous 12 months and more than 150k businesses, mainly in Spain and the UK. It later raised €13.7M from NYC fund 14W — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2016/11/10/job-today-bags-20m-to-grow-its-service-industry-hiring-app-in-europe-and-beyond/?ncid=rss); [Capital Riesgo](https://capital-riesgo.es/en/articles/job-today-capta-13-7-millones-del-fondo-neoyorquino-14w-/)

**Hirect (India; chat-first direct hiring for startups)**
- Laid off about 40% of staff (around 200 people) in 2022, citing restructuring and a business-model change amid the startup hiring slowdown. Headcount had peaked at about 600 — [HRKatha](https://www.hrkatha.com/hiring-firing/hirect-fires-40-workforce-as-part-of-strategic-changes/); [India.com](https://www.india.com/business/organisational-restructure-hiring-platform-hirect-fires-40-employees-5770517/)

**Lunchclub (US; AI-matched 1:1 professional "coffee chats")**
- Backed by a16z, Lightspeed and Coatue, with a 2020 Series B reported at $100M+ valuation — [linkedjn page (low quality)](https://linkedjn.it.com/lunchclub.html); [South Park Commons](https://southparkcommons.com/companies/lunchclub)
- Reportedly raised undisclosed funding on 2025-11-11, co-led by Founders Fund and Ribbit; CEO Vladimir Novakovski. No shutdown found — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/elliot-technologies/people); [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/lighter/343127)

**TheLadders**: launched a "Tinder for recruiters" swipe app (about 2015) — [The Muse](https://www.themuse.com/advice/tinder-for-recruiters-check-out-theladders-new-app)

**New wave (2024–26)**: Sorce markets itself as "Tinder for jobs" with AI auto-apply and 40 free swipes a day. Reception is mixed — [Sorce](https://www.sorce.jobs/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tinder-jobs-app-claims-apply-173148641.html); [AutoApplier review](https://www.autoapplier.com/blog/tinder-for-jobs)

### Cited Findings: why they failed
- **Thin, non-fungible pools.** Dating works because users want broadly similar things and the pool is large and liquid. Specialized roles (e.g., a biotech CFO) aren't like that; the model fits commoditized labor like home care — [Dan McCarthy on X](https://x.com/dmccarthy7/status/1852420836415963315); [Jobtrain blog](https://jobtrain.co.uk/blog/switch-and-jobr-tinder-for-jobs)
- **Low added value.** Online applying is already convenient and private, so swiping removes little friction — [Dan McCarthy on X](https://x.com/dmccarthy7/status/1852420836415963315)
- **Recruiter burden and poor screening.** Candidates were recommended jobs far off-level, and recruiters had to sift through unsuitable swipes. There was no evidence that Jobr or Switch used screening questions — [Jobtrain blog](https://jobtrain.co.uk/blog/switch-and-jobr-tinder-for-jobs)
- **Supply-side cold start.** Too few recruiters posted on Jobr, so it backfilled with Indeed API listings, which turned it into a mobile reskin of aggregator content — [Jobtrain blog](https://jobtrain.co.uk/blog/switch-and-jobr-tinder-for-jobs)
- **Platform dependency.** LinkedIn's API lockdown hurt Jobr — [Jobtrain blog](https://jobtrain.co.uk/blog/switch-and-jobr-tinder-for-jobs)
- **Swiping doesn't change pipelines.** Recruiters still review in batches and favor early applicants — [AutoApplier](https://www.autoapplier.com/blog/tinder-for-jobs)
- **Gimmick critique** from the recruiting industry: "Can we just stop with the 'Tinder for jobs'…" — [Chad & Cheese 2017](https://www.chadcheese.com/post/2017/11/05/can-we-just-stop-with-the-tinder-for-jobs-and-the-video-resumes-already-please)

### Inferences
- The pattern: exit came through acquisition by an incumbent (Monster/Jobr, Adecco/Hired, Lincoln/Shapr) or a feature being dropped (Bumble Bizz). No pure swipe-to-hire app became a durable independent company.
- Where it worked better, the domain was **high-volume, fungible, local hourly work** (Job Today, Hirect's chat-first model), or the product dropped swiping in favor of **curation** (Hired, Lunchclub).
- Hooked's event-scoped density is a plausible counter to the cold-start/thin-pool problem. A conference concentrates relevant, intent-filtered candidates and recruiters in one place and time.

### Gaps
- I found no formal post-mortems from Switch or Jobr founders. Switch's shutdown date and reason are unconfirmed. The Jobr price ($12.5M) is unverified.
- Current status of Job Today (any acquisition after 2019?) is not confirmed. Hirect's current status and download numbers were not found.
- "Woo" (Indian anonymous tech-hiring app) and "Emjoy" were not researched due to the tool-call budget. Emjoy appears to be a women's wellness app and probably not relevant. Blind's coffee-chat features were not covered.

## Q3. Any Israeli startups that tried "Tinder for jobs"?

### Takeaway
Yes:
- **Workey** (Tel Aviv, founded 2014) launched a "Tinder for recruitment" model with anonymous matching in 2017 and raised about $9.6M in total. Its current status is unknown.
- **JobSwipe** (founded 2014 by Ron Ozery) is listed in Start-Up Nation Central with a Tinder-like swipe UI.
- **Switch**'s founder Yarden Tadmor is reportedly Israeli-born, but Switch was a NYC company.

### Cited Findings
- Workey was founded in 2014 by Unit 8200 veterans Ben Reuveni and Amichai Schreiber. It raised a $1.6M seed, then an $8M Series A (June 2017; PICO Partners and Magma VC lead), opened a New York office, and reported more than 400 client companies — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/workey-raises-8-million-sets-eyes-on-us-market/); [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2017/06/06/workey-funding/); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/workey-raises-8-million)
- Workey's mechanics:
  - a chatbot interviews passive candidates about their goals and surfaces relevant roles for them to swipe
  - recruiters see anonymized profiles
  - a mutual match triggers an introduction
  
  TechCrunch headline: "Workey launches 'Tinder for recruitment'" (2017-10-26). The Forward reported about 500 corporate users, mostly tech, and a low App Store rating driven by UX complaints — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/10/26/workey/); [Forward](https://forward.com/life/387009/swipe-right-on-your-dream-job-this-israeli-made-app-is-tinder-for-job/)
- JobSwipe: founded January 2014 by Ron Ozery; it matches candidates to jobs via a Tinder-like UI — [Start-Up Nation Finder](https://finder.startupnationcentral.org/company_page/jobswipe)
- Hebrew coverage of Workey exists in Walla and Bizportal — [Walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/2935219); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/advertismentandmarketing/news/article/418957)

### Inferences
- Workey's anonymity-until-mutual-match design targeted passive, already-employed Israeli tech talent. That is the same audience that attends professional conferences.
- I found no news on Workey after 2017, which suggests it went quiet. This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- Current status of Workey and JobSwipe (shut down or acquired?) was not found. The Israeli Companies Registrar was not checked.
- No Geektime/Calcalist/Globes post-mortems surfaced, and Hebrew searches returned irrelevant results.
- I found no Israeli app that combines conference/event QR check-in with hiring matches.
