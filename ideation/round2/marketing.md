# Round 2: Marketing stress-test of Concept v2

## 1. The three biggest flaws

1. **The whole funnel depends on one email from a brand nobody knows.** Recruiters buy because of pre-event matching. That needs employed engineers to opt in 72 hours early, from an organizer email, to a brand with no history. If 3% opt in, recruiters see "4 people" and the ₪3,500 sale dies. **The pre-event candidate campaign is the product.**
2. **The privacy promises contradict each other.** v2 says:
   - data dies after 72 hours, but the north star is measured over 14 days
   - there is ATS export and a story engine
   - you're in "only because you're physically here", yet profiles open before the event

   One screenshot of a broken promise in a dev WhatsApp group would kill us. Make one auditable promise: **"Nothing about you leaves the event unless you say yes to a specific company."**
3. **Sponsor-first selling looks like "the organizer sold me to its sponsors".** Sponsors get 24-hour early access and are the first buyers. To a candidate that looks like attendee data being sold. Sell sponsors *first pick of coffee slots and a branded corner*, never "access to candidates". Also, the required salary band is our best candidate hook, but it will cost us some companies. Show bands only inside the event app, never on a public page.

## 2. Tension #5 (Hooked) and earning candidate trust

**One learning coffee with Noa Barazani. No license, partnership or co-brand in year 1.**
- **Why not partner:** the dating association is the very trust problem we're solving, in front of HR legal and in front of employed engineers. Hooked's assets (QR entry, a "who's here" list) are the cheap part; Tech scoped them at 8–10 weeks. Recruiter sales and the Employer Shield are the expensive parts, and Hooked has neither.
- **What to ask her:** what share of attendees actually scan the QR, and which activation tactics work. That is the number BizDev's 35% assumption rests on.
- **Starting today:** stop saying "Hooked for jobs" in anything external. It risks a passing-off complaint and puts the wrong picture in people's heads.

**Trust when we have no track record:**
1. **Co-brand, don't white-label:** "Hosted by DevOpsDays TLV · powered by Hallway".
2. **Show, don't claim:** a live preview of "what companies see before you knock: nothing", and "Your employer: [Company] blocked" shown right after you enter it.
3. **Real people:** the founders' faces and phone number, plus 3 respected Israeli platform leads who vouch publicly.
4. **Deletion in writing:** a Hebrew "privacy in 5 lines" reviewed by a lawyer, and a WhatsApp deletion receipt.
5. **A no-ghosting pledge from companies:** a reply within 48 hours, or the candidate is told and the company is flagged.
6. **Seed with people who have little to hide:** returning reservists, olim, people moving back from abroad.

## 3. Naming: final recommendation

**Spoken brand "Hallway". Registered mark "Hallway Talent". Hebrew sub-brand "מסדרון" (Misderon).**
- **Hallway:** a generic word with small unrelated users: a Sequoia Arc-backed avatar/virtual-meetings startup in Missouri (about 5 people), Hallway Studios, and The Hallway, a Sydney agency. I found no recruiting/HR product called Hallway (only a 2023 student pitch). So the standalone mark is weak, but confusion risk in HR services is low. Register the composite.
- **Misderon / מסדרון:** no app or brand found.
- **Domains:** the sandbox blocked registrar/RDAP lookups, so check these by hand and buy them before any PR, in this order: hallway.jobs, hallwaytalent.com, hallway.co.il, misderon.co.il.
- **Product vocabulary:** Knock, Coffee Slot, Employer Shield, Hallway Pass. "Knock" is fine for a feature but too crowded for a brand.

## 5. Where I still disagree

- **The beachhead is spread too thin.** v2 lists five events across three niches. I'd run **one niche (DevOps/platform/SRE) for 3 events**, so the same companies, the same candidates and the stories compound. The Cyber Week lounge should wait until year 2.
- **The story engine isn't a v1 feature.** It's marketing work done by hand: told by the employer, with consent, 90 days after the hire.
- **The booth stunt at event 3** only works once we have hire stories behind it. Without them it gets mocked.
- **Mid-level 3–8 years:** I accept it as the targeting, but the brand voice stays peer-to-peer and senior.
- **72 hours vs 14 days:** keep one public promise. Measure outcomes only through opted-in surveys.
