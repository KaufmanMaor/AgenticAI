# Round 2 — BizDev stress test of Concept v2 (Hallway)

*Rate used: ₪3.7 = $1. Anything marked **[A]** is my assumption.*

## 1. The three biggest flaws in v2

1. **Companies have to pay before they know any candidates exist.** The ≥5-prepaid gate asks companies to pay ₪3,500 before they can see a single candidate. In Knock mode they may see none until someone knocks. HR's round-1 condition was "14 people matched and agreed to meet, 48 hours before." v2 asks for the money first and only promises that proof later. **Fix:** an **intro guarantee**: at least 5 held intros or a full credit toward the next event. Also, the 72-hour pre-event preview has to happen before the pass is final (a pre-sale with a refund window).
2. **The people buying aren't the people hiring.** "Sponsors first" is half right. Sponsors of dev conferences skew toward **dev-tool vendors selling to developers** (DevOpsDays TLV 2024's top tiers were ScyllaDB, LinearB, Couchbase and AWS). Their booth staff are sales and DevRel, not hiring managers. The biggest hirers (Nvidia, Wix, monday, Wiz) often skip small community conferences. **Fix:** sell to two pools: (a) sponsors that also hire, and (b) heavy hirers who aren't sponsors. Pool (b) gets passes through the organizer, which also gives organizers a new paying customer.
3. **There's too little in it for organizers, and the volunteer ones don't care.** 25% of 5 passes is about ₪4.4K. A commercial organizer won't risk upsetting a ₪60K sponsor for that, and community organizers (Reversim, DevOpsDays) aren't driven by revenue at all. On top of that, **v2 deletes data at 72 hours but measures success at 14 days**: we can't produce the ROI report from data we've already deleted. **Fix:** for community events, the rev share goes to the community (food, scholarships). For commercial organizers, the real pitch is **"sponsors renew because the booth now has ROI"**, with Hallway Pass bundled into sponsor tiers. Keep data only with consent from both sides when they choose "keep in touch". Everything else is deleted at 72 hours.

## 2. Open tensions

**#2: Are 5 prepaid companies realistic? Yes, if we sell from the sponsor list.** DevOpsDays TLV 2024 listed around 30+ Gold-tier sponsors. Converting 15–20% of them gives 5–6 companies. Add 2–3 non-sponsor hirers that the organizer approves **[A]**. The candidate experience is the real risk. A board with 5 logos looks thin, so require **≥3 role cards per company, all matched to the conference topic** (DevOps/SRE/platform at DevOpsDays). The pre-event email then says **"22 open roles at 6 companies, salary bands shown"**, which sounds better than "6 companies." The deadline for hitting the gate is T-21 days. If we miss it, we cancel and refund. We never run an event with an empty board.

**#3: What if a sponsor demands exclusivity?** Never sell hiring exclusivity. A single-company board is just a booth, and it loses the candidates. What we offer instead:
- **Presenting Hiring Partner** (₪20–30K, one per event): naming rights on the Coffee corner, 24 hours of early access, first choice of intro slots, and their logo in the attendee email.
- **Employer Shield**, which deals with the fear behind the demand: the sponsor's own employees are never visible to competitors unless they knock on that competitor themselves.
- If a sponsor still insists, the price for exclusivity is **₪60K** **[A]** (8 passes we can't sell + a premium + the cost to our candidate brand). If they won't pay that, we skip that event.

## 3. Unit economics for event #1 (concierge, 1,200 attendees, ₪3,500 pass)

| Line item | ₪ |
|---|---|
| 6 Hallway Passes × ₪3,500 | 21,000 |
| Organizer rev share 25% (pilot) | -5,250 |
| 3 matchmakers × 2 days (prep + event day) | -6,000 |
| Coffee corner: furniture, signage, coffee | -3,000 |
| WhatsApp, forms, printing | -500 |
| **Contribution before founder time** | **~6,250** |
| Founder sales time (~3 weeks, opportunity cost) | about -6,000 |

**Funnel [A]:** 1,200 attendees, 12% build a profile = 145. Of those, 70 are mid-level and relevant. **48 intros held** (8 per company). **40% book a next step = ~19.** That's 1–2 hires within 60 days.

**What a company pays:** ₪3,500 ÷ 8 intros = **₪440 per intro**. That's just above HR's ₪200–400 per meeting, so we need 10 or more intros for it to feel like a good deal. **Event #1 breaks even at best. It's for learning, not profit.** It pays off when there are **8 passes plus one Presenting Partner (~₪50K gross)**.

## 4. Pitches

**Email to the organizer (one paragraph):**
> Hi [Name], we're piloting Hallway at Israeli dev conferences: a private "who's hiring here" board where your attendees browse roles with salary bands and book a 15-minute coffee with the hiring manager. Their employer never sees them. We run everything on-site (a coffee corner and 3 staff), with no integration and no app to install. Your sponsors finally get booth ROI they can report, and non-sponsor hiring companies become new paying customers through you. Can we take 20 minutes to plan a pilot at [Event]? If fewer than 5 companies commit by T-21, we don't run it, and it costs you nothing.

**Organizer deal terms:**
| Term | Pilot (event 1) | Ongoing |
|---|---|---|
| Fee to organizer | 0 | 0 |
| Rev share | 25% of passes (to the community fund if a community event) | 25% passes, 15% Presenting Partner |
| Organizer gives | Coffee-corner space, 1 attendee email at T-72h, the sponsor list for outreach, approval of non-sponsor companies | + Hallway Pass as a line in sponsor tiers |
| We give | Staff, the board, attendee data controls, a post-event report | + co-marketing, priority dates |
| Control | Organizer can veto any company and choose sponsor-only mode | Same |
| Data | Organizer gets aggregate stats only, no candidate PII | Same |
| Term | Per event, no exclusivity | 12-month first refusal on their series |

**Pitch to companies:** "Eight 15-minute coffees with mid-level engineers in your stack, who chose you, salary aligned, at a conference you already attend. ₪3,500, with 5 intros guaranteed or full credit."

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We already have a booth." | The booth gets you swag hunters. This sends you the engineers who would never walk up to it. |
| "Candidates are invisible. What am I paying for?" | You see the anonymous demand count 72 hours before. If fewer than 5 intros happen, you pay nothing. |
| "Our hiring manager can't give up a day." | Two hours is enough. The slots are pre-booked. |
| "They'll poach *our* people." | Employer Shield: your employees are never shown to anyone they haven't knocked on. |
| "We already have them in Comeet." | That's fine. There's no success fee, so there's no attribution fight. Export them with one tap. |
| "Salary band is mandatory?" | Yes. It's what gets the knocks. Roles without a band get about 0 [A]. |
