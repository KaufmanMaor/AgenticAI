# Concept v2 — Founder synthesis after Round 1

*Written by the founder after reading the round-1 inputs (PM, Marketing, BizDev, Tech, HR). This is the version the team now stress-tests.*

## What I'm keeping from my original idea
- **Event-only, presence-gated, ephemeral**, the way Hooked works: you're in only because you're physically at this event (rotating QR at the venue), and the data dies 72 hours later unless both sides choose to keep in touch.
- **Humans talking, not an AI filter.** AI helps you write a profile in 2 minutes. It never scores, ranks or rejects anyone.
- **Meet today.** The value is a real conversation at the venue, not another application.

## What I'm changing (the team convinced me)
1. **Visibility is asymmetric, not symmetric.** Hiring is public and job seeking is risky. So **companies are visible and candidates are invisible by default.**
   - **Knock mode (default):** the candidate browses a live "who's hiring here" board (companies, roles, salary band, the person in the room) and knocks. Nobody sees the candidate until they knock, and only that company sees them.
   - **Open mode (opt-in):** verified recruiters can see an anonymized card (role, seniority, skills, no name, no photo, no employer). The candidate's own employer and its group companies are always blocked automatically, and when in doubt we block.
2. **Coffee intro, not "interview."** A 15-minute structured intro conversation in a "Hallway Coffee" corner, and the next step has to be booked before both people leave. It's lower risk legally and less scary for employed people.
3. **Hiring-manager-led.** Each paying company has to bring someone who *does the job* (team lead or hiring manager), not only HR. Senior people talk to their peers.
4. **Pre-event matching + live on-site.** Profiles open 72 hours before the event via the organizer's email. Recruiters see "N relevant people are coming (anonymized)" and pre-book slots. On the day, live knocks fill the gaps. This is what makes a recruiter buy.
5. **Drop the dating language.** The working name is **Hallway** ("The hallway track, with intent"); Misderon is the Hebrew option. The Hooked story is told once, in launch PR.
6. **The remote video booth is postponed.** v1.5 is "remote company slots" by phone video in a quiet room. The booth becomes a stunt at event 3, and a real product only in year 2.

## Who pays and how
- **Candidates:** always free.
- **Companies:** **Hallway Pass** per event, ₪3,500 (about $950). The first buyers are companies **already exhibiting or sponsoring**, since they're in the room anyway and can't prove ROI on the booth. Remote companies pay ₪2,000 for video slots.
- **Organizers:** distribution partner with a 25% rev share, plus a "Hiring Partner" sponsorship for a branded Coffee corner. Sponsors can get 24-hour early access.
- **Later:** an annual plan (about $6–9K a year) across all events, priced below a LinkedIn Recruiter seat. No success fee in year 1 (attribution disputes, and it risks being treated as a regulated placement agency).

## Beachhead
Israeli developer, AI and cyber conferences: DevOpsDays TLV, Reversim, Geektime Code, AI Dev TLV, then the Cyber Week lounge. Mid-level roles (3–8 years of experience).

## Product v1
- QR-launched web app (no install) and WhatsApp notifications
- CV upload → an LLM drafts 5 fields → the user edits them
- Rotating venue QR to prove presence
- Automatic employer blocking
- Company and role cards with a required salary band
- Knock/Open modes and slot booking
- One-tap export to Comeet/ATS (with consent)
- A post-event ROI report for the company and a "Hired in the Hallway" story engine

## Run rules (gates)
- No event without **≥5 prepaid companies** that each bring a hiring manager.
- First 2–3 events run as a **manual concierge** (Google Form + WhatsApp + human matchmakers) before we write production code.
- North star: **qualified intros with a booked next step within 14 days**. Guardrail: **zero candidate exposures to their own employer.**

## Open tensions I want the team to stress-test
1. Does Knock mode give recruiters enough? If candidates are invisible, will companies still pay ₪3,500?
2. Are 5 prepaid companies at a 1,000–1,500-person Israeli dev conference realistic? What does that do to the candidate experience?
3. Sponsors vs competitors: what if the main sponsor demands exclusivity?
4. Amendment 13: is job-seeking status "sensitive data"? What is the minimum compliant setup for a concierge pilot?
5. Should we approach Hooked (Noa Barazani) for a partnership or license, or stay clear?
6. What is the moat in 24 months?
