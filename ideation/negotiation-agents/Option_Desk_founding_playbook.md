# Option Desk: Founding Playbook

*Output of a 3-round ideation on Omri Bitan's idea ("AI agents that negotiate real-estate deals for you"). Team: PM, Marketing, BizDev, CTO, Austin broker, Texas RE attorney. US only, Texas first. 2026-11-20. Inputs: `research_notes/סוכני AI למשא ומתן בנדלן/`, `reports/סוכני AI למשא ומתן בנדלן.md`, `ideation/negotiation-agents/round1-3/`.*

## 0. Verdict
**All six roles signed off, each with conditions. All six answered "yes, worth the next 12 months", framed as a 13–16-week kill-or-continue bet (~$50–150K) that must earn the remaining 9 months.** The pain is real and expensive: ~15% of US contracts cancel (17–22% in Texas metros), led by inspection and repair disputes, and a dead deal costs the buyer agent ~$10.8K. There is a legal path, and AI now negotiates at human level. The original vision (two AI agents, one per side, closing between them) does **not** survive. It is unlicensed brokerage in Texas, a Texas intermediary deal if both agents sit under one company, the stronger model quietly wins, and the counterparty is a human listing agent with a phone.

## 1. What changed from Omri's idea
| Omri's version | Option Desk |
|---|---|
| An agent for each side, bot-to-bot | **Buyer side only**, bot-to-human; the listing agent is a person |
| The agent does the whole negotiation | **One moment:** the Texas option period after inspection (TREC 39 amendment) |
| The agent closes the deal | **The AI drafts, a licensee sends, only the principals sign** |
| Sold to consumers | **Sold to buyer agents** (88% of buyers have one) as a back office |
| Win price | **Save the deal**, then net value for the buyer |
| Agents talking to agents | Long-term **protocol across brokerages**, probed with iBuyers (Opendoor/Offerpad) |

## 2. The product
A **"negotiation transaction coordinator"** for Texas buyer agents. Upload the inspection report and get back, **the same evening, 7 days a week** (received by 4pm, returned by 10pm):
1. **A priced, prioritized ask list** from the Austin price book, each item citing its report page. Foundation, roof and sewer get a range plus a **contractor bid within 48h**.
2. **One recommended ask plus 1–2 alternatives** in a single message. The seller's pick reveals their priorities.
3. **A call script** for the "one more phone call" (the broker's insight: the real edge is off the record).
4. **The TREC 39 amendment**, generated by code from structured terms after the seller picks, sent via DocuSign.
5. **A counter reader** that proposes the next move.
6. **An option clock:** the licensee confirms the Effective Date at intake, a backup approver is named, there are escalations at 48h, 24h and 6h, and a Notice of Termination is pre-drafted. The licensee sends it, only on the buyer's documented instruction.

**Safety:** walk-away limits stay out of the model's context. PDFs are rendered to images and OCR'd. There is an outbound leak filter with canaries. The AI has no signing credentials, and every message carries a non-binding footer. We describe this as "injection-resistant, tested each release."

**Disclosure** (email): *"Prepared with AI-assisted drafting (Option Desk). Reviewed and sent by [Name], TREC #[x], [Brokerage], who represents the Buyer. Non-binding proposal; no agreement until a written amendment is signed by all parties."* Texts carry a short tag: *"AI-assisted · sent by [Name], [Brokerage]."*

## 3. Structure and legal
- **The Texas broker co-founder is the designated broker** from month 0 (10–15% equity, compliance veto). The company holds a TREC license, which makes broker-to-broker payments clean under §1101.651.
- **A broker-to-broker services agreement:** we serve the sponsoring broker, we represent no party, and our staff have **zero contact** with the buyer or the counterparty. Advice reaches the buyer only through the licensee.
- **Fee:** fixed per file, never tied to closing, never billed to the buyer. **The agent pays by card, and the sponsoring broker approves once** in a master agreement.
- **Data:** buyer consent sits in the agent's client form, with a defined anonymization standard. Use by any future brokerage of ours is contractually barred.
- **Insurance:** tech E&O plus brokerage E&O, **with $1M budgeted** in case the designated broker's stake drops below 10% at seed (to verify).
- **Before file 1:** a ~$3K attorney memo on four questions: (1) is our licensed company "providing brokerage services to the buyer"; (2) the flat fee; (3) the concierge; (4) the disclosure wording. Optionally, an informal question to TREC staff.

## 4. Business model
- **List price $249 per file.** Pilot pricing is a **refundable 3-file pack at $399**. Team pack: 10 files for $1,990.
- **The ceiling is honest:** about 250K option-period files a year in Texas means ~$6–12M ARR at a 10–20% share; the US is ~$40M at 5%. **Per-file SaaS alone isn't venture-scale.** The seed story needs an expansion path:
  1. Texas-wide in year 1.
  2. Appraisal-gap and listing-side files.
  3. Enterprise licenses to teams and brokerages.
  4. A priced-outcome data product.
  5. **The month-12 A-vs-B decision:** our own buyer brokerage (in a different metro, separate brand, firewalled) or the iBuyer protocol.
- **Moat:** priced-outcome data (which asks get accepted, at what price, by which listing-agent type).
- **Money:** pre-seed of $0.75–1M now, then a $3–4M seed after the gate.

## 5. Plan: 16 weeks to a kill-or-continue decision
| Weeks | Work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Broker co-founder, TREC entity, attorney memo, paid contractor panel (~$3K) and price book, concierge stack (~$4.5K: Claude extraction, Sheet price book, pypdf TREC 39, DocuSign, Twilio clock, Airtable log) | Legal green light |
| 1–3 | **Validation before any live file:** on 15 historical replay files, the simulator and a 3-broker panel must predict the real seller response within ±25% in ≥60% of files. The price book must hit ≥70% of items within the contractor range and ≥98% recall on structural items. | Gate tools trusted |
| 2–5 | Recruit **15–20 Austin agents or teams** ("Bring us a dead deal" blind replay; pitch at 2 team meetings: "send us your next inspection report") | **Pre-sale test:** ≥6 buy the $399 pack. **Fewer than 4 → stop.** |
| 4–15 | 30 live concierge files run by the licensed broker. Harness, price book and clock built in parallel. | Data |
| 16 | Gate readout | Build v1 (12 weeks, 3 engineers) and raise, or kill |
| 3–24 | Parallel probe: one **LOI with Opendoor/Offerpad** by month 6, no engineering | Agent-to-agent path |

## 6. Gate (all must pass at file 30 / week 16)
- **(a) Value:** compare the accepted outcome per file with the agent's **historical realization** on matched past files. Mean incremental net value ≥ $3K, with a paired 95% CI lower bound > $0. **Guardrail:** option-period deaths do not rise. If the week 1–3 validation fails, fall back to a broker-panel estimate with κ ≥ 0.5.
- **(b) Usability:** ≥ 70% of drafts sent with light edits.
- **(c) Willingness to pay:** the pre-sale test plus ≥ 60% of pilot agents buying a second pack.
- **(d) Quality:** the blind 3-broker panel prefers our drafts ≥ 65% of the time.
- **(e) Ops:** concierge expert time ≤ 90 minutes per file and falling. Same-evening turnaround is hit ≥ 90% of the time.

**Kill if:** (a) or (c) fails; there is a TREC complaint, a missed option deadline caused by us, or a reservation-price leak; or the attorney memo blocks the structure.

## 7. Top risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Agents admire the drafts but won't send live files mid-crunch, or won't pay again (an option period comes about once a month) | Pre-sale pack, team-lead buyers, recruit 15–20 not 10 |
| The value can't be proven honestly | Validated simulator and panel, historical realization baseline, survival guardrail |
| TREC treats us as serving the buyer | Broker-to-broker agreement, zero buyer contact, attorney memo |
| KW Command, Compass or Lofty ship it as a feature | Speed to Texas-wide coverage, outcome-data moat, an independent-agent focus |
| The listing side ignores or punishes AI-labeled counters | Sent under the agent's name, one ask plus alternatives, a phone script, tracking engagement by listing-agent type |
| Low ceiling | The expansion path and the month-12 A/B decision are part of the seed pitch |
