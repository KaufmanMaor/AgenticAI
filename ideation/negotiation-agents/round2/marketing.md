# Round 2: Marketing / Growth stress test of Concept v2

*Role: Head of Marketing / Growth. I read concept_v2_founder_synthesis.md and every Round 1 file. My Round 1 plan was consumer-first; v2 sells B2B to buyer agents first, so much of my Round 1 GTM is retired (see §7).*

## 1. The 3 biggest flaws in v2 (from the Marketing seat)
1. **Phase 2 betrays the Phase 1 channel.** For six months we court Austin buyer agents, learn their files and earn their trust. Then we launch **Punchlist Realty**, a 1%-rebate brokerage that competes for *their* buyers, built on *their* data. In an Austin agent Facebook group, that story reads "they used us to train their competitor," and it kills Phase 1 revenue and the listing-side goodwill Phase 2 depends on. **Fix:** a hard brand and data firewall. The agent tool gets its own brand. A written promise that agent file data never feeds Phase 2 lead targeting. Phase 2 goes after segments agents don't serve well: relocation, builder buyers and self-represented buyers. No poaching campaigns, ever.
2. **"Free for the first 20 files" tests value but not willingness to pay.** The file-30 gate measures dollars and survival, then assumes $249 sticks. Agents love free concierge work. **Fix:** file 1 free, file 2 onward at $249 (or $149 "Founding Agent" pricing locked for 12 months). Add a third gate metric: **≥60% of pilot agents pay for a second file.**
3. **The proof can't be marketed.** "Blind replay vs. past files" is fine as research. It is useless as a sales claim, and the attorney's red line bans "guaranteed savings" advertising. Phase 2's "negotiation guarantee" collides with that same line. **Fix:** market *process facts* the agent can verify on their own file: "ready to send in 2 hours," "seller's-choice package," "contractor-backed numbers," "deadline alarms at 48/24/6h." Phase 2 frames the guarantee as a **rebate schedule** ("1% back, rising to 2% if post-inspection credits come in under $3K"), not guaranteed savings, pending review under TAC §535.155.

*Opportunity v2 misses:* every package we send lands on a **listing agent's** desk. That is a free, high-trust impression on our next customer. The design should make the listing agent think "I want my buyer-side deals to look like this." That is our viral loop. A small "powered by" line is worth more than any ad.

## 2. Tensions #1 and #3
**#1: Don't insult the agent.** We never sell "negotiate better." We sell **time, a calm client and a saved commission**. The frame is *back office*, the same as a transaction coordinator: "Your TC handles dates and docs; Option Desk handles the inspection response." Rules:
- **Words we use:** prep, package, desk, ready to send, your numbers, your call. **Words we ban:** coach, train, improve your negotiation, better than, AI negotiator.
- **Price anchor:** $249 is less than most Austin TCs charge per file (assumption, verify), and the commission at risk is about $10K. "One saved deal pays for 40 files."
- **The agent gets the credit.** The buyer-facing report is **white-labeled with the agent's name, logo and headshot**. Our brand appears only as a small "powered by." Agents pay for things that win reviews and referrals.

**#3: Disclosure label (exact wording).** The label is required (attorney red line: no undisclosed bot), so the question is placement and tone, not whether. The human comes first and the disclosure sits in the footer, matter-of-fact. Never "AI-generated" or "AI-negotiated." Never put "AI" in the subject line.
> *Footer, every email, text and PDF:* **"Prepared with AI-assisted drafting (Option Desk). Reviewed and sent by Jane Rivera, Lone Star Realty Group, TREC #0000000. This message is not an offer or acceptance; terms bind only in a TREC amendment signed by the parties."**
> *SMS short form:* **"AI-assisted draft, reviewed & sent by Jane Rivera (TREC #0000000)."**
Test **placement and wording only**, never on vs. off. Metrics: listing-agent reply time, phone-call rate and amendment-signed rate vs. the agent's own past files. If listing agents call the buyer agent, that's fine: the human answers. That is the design, not a failure.

## 3. Positioning and one-liner for agents
**Positioning:** For Austin buyer agents with deals in the option period, **Option Desk** turns the inspection report into a priced, seller's-choice repair amendment the same evening. You keep the relationship and the final call. We do the 3 hours of work at 9pm, so the deal survives and your client thinks you're a genius.
**One-liner:** **"Inspection report in, ready-to-send amendment out, before your buyer panics."**
Secondary lines: "Save the deal, keep your evening." / "The inspection response listing agents actually answer."

## 4. Recruiting the first 10 Austin agents in 3 weeks
**Target:** solo and small-team buyer agents with 6-25 buyer sides a year (the broker's sweet spot), ideally with 2+ deals in or near option right now. **Funnel:** 150 named agents → 60 conversations → 20 "dead-deal replays" → 10 signed.
- **Week 1: build the list and start warm outreach.** Pull agents with 6-25 closed buyer sides in the last 12 months from public MLS-derived profiles. Ask our broker co-founder for 30 warm intros. Ask 10 friendly inspectors *which agents call them at 10pm* (introductions only, no payment of any kind, per the RESPA red line). Ask 3-5 independent TCs, who see every agent's pain.
- **Week 2: the "Bring Us a Dead Deal" replay.** The agent sends the inspection report from a deal they lost or nearly lost. Within 24 hours we return the package we would have sent. This is the hook, the demo and the measurement baseline in one, and it is our strongest asset.
- **Week 3: sign and go live.** A Founding Agent deal: first file free, then $149 per file locked for 12 months, a direct line to the founders, and their name on the "Founding Agents" page if they want it. The goal is one live file per agent within 10 days.
- **Rooms:** ABoR CE classes and team meetings (offer a 20-minute "Option-period fall-throughs in 2026" talk with data, no pitch). Austin agent Facebook groups, but only once we have 3 testimonials. Never cold-post before then.

**Call opener (broker or founder):** "Quick one. How many deals did you lose in the option period last year? ... We built a desk that turns the inspection report into a priced, seller's-choice amendment in two hours. You stay the negotiator. Send me one report from a deal that died, and tomorrow I'll show you what we'd have sent. No cost."

**Sample DM (Instagram/LinkedIn/text):**

We're a small Austin team building an "option desk" for buyer agents. ## 5. Phase 2 consumer brand: does "Punchlist" work for both phases?
**No. Use two brands with a firewall.** If Phase 1 footers say "Punchlist" on every listing agent's screen, Phase 2's "Punchlist Realty" will look like the tool vendor turned competitor (flaw #1).
- **Phase 1 (B2B):** **Option Desk**, a plain utility name that agents can say out loud. Its output is white-labeled to the agent. "Option Day" stays a seasonal agent campaign.
- **Phase 2 (consumer):** **Punchlist** (Punchlist Realty plus the free "Punchlist: inspection → ask list" tool). It is concrete, about the house, and good for Austin consumer press and the **Austin Repair Price Index** (aggregate, anonymized data, pending the attorney's confidentiality OK).
- **Bridge:** one parent company, disclosed honestly. But the Phase 2 promise in writing is "we never solicit buyers of agents who use Option Desk" (BizDev and attorney to check feasibility). Option Desk continues as a licensing line for brokerages outside Austin, where Punchlist Realty doesn't compete.
- Trademark: run Punchlist in Class 36 (real estate) vs. construction punch-list apps (Class 9/42) before spending a dollar.

## 7. What I accept or reject from the other roles
- **Accept, PM:** buyer agent first, concierge before build, north star = option-period survival. My consumer-first beachhead is retired.
- **Accept, Broker:** bot-to-human, a seller's-choice menu, "make the listing agent's job easy," and $150-300 per transaction. The brokerage-office license at $5-15K/year is our month 6-12 upsell.
- **Accept, Attorney:** disclosure in every message, no "guaranteed savings" ads, no inspector or lender referral payments. This **kills my Round 1 consumer money-back guarantee and paid inspector referrals.** Inspectors become intro sources only, or a flat-fee software integration (Spectora/ISN).
- **Accept, Tech:** the dual-LLM split and a policy engine for limits. That makes "your walk-away never enters the AI" a true, marketable line.
- **Reject, BizDev:** "B insults the agent, so skip to the brokerage." The insult is a framing problem (§2) that we can fix. Skipping straight to a brokerage puts us against Homa's 2% at a ~$7K CAC with no proof. Accept BizDev's "1% + guarantee," but only as the reworded rebate schedule.
- **Reject, my Round 1:** consumer launch stunts. The "Break the Ceiling" $1,000 bounty moves to a Phase 2 engineering-PR slot. **Keep:** the Repair Price Index (agents will share it with clients) and the banned-words list.
- **Revised 90-day targets:** 10 Founding Agents by week 3, 30 live files by day 60 (the gate), **≥60% of agents paying for a second file**, 40 agents / 120 paid files by day 90, a listing-agent reply time ≤ the agent's baseline, and 3 inbound sign-ups from listing agents who received our packages.
