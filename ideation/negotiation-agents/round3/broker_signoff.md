# Round 3: Broker sign-off (Austin TX broker seat)

**(a) SIGN-OFF WITH CONDITIONS.** v3 fixes my three Round 2 flaws (same-metro channel conflict, the dollar guarantee, the missing turnaround promise) and the structure is TREC-clean. Two conditions: fix the gate math, and get bids lined up before file 1.

**(b) The biggest remaining risk: agents won't route *live* files to a new vendor in the middle of a 5-7 day option crunch.** Blind replays of dead deals are easy to get; a live inspection report at 9pm on day 3 is not. Pilot volume is the related problem. A solo buyer agent writes about 1 contract a month, so 10 agents over 8 weeks give roughly 15-20 files, not 30. And with 2 free files per agent, gate (c) rests on a handful of paid files.
- *Cheapest test (~$0, 3 weeks, before building anything):* pitch two Austin team meetings (30-40 agents) with "send us your next inspection report, free." Count how many send a live report within 21 days, and how many hours after the inspection they send it. Under 8 live files means recruit 20 agents or teams, not 10.
- *Second cheap test:* send 5 past reports to the contractor panel now and time foundation, roof and HVAC bids. If bids take more than 48h, the 4-hour promise covers only the easy items.

**(c) Inconsistencies in v3**
1. **Gate (a) compares asks, not outcomes.** Only one ask goes to the seller, so a "paired incremental net value" against the agent's sealed draft can't be observed. Measure ask quality on the replay panel (d), and measure outcomes as the seller's first response vs. the final amendment.
2. **"Ask list in 4 business hours" vs. "foundation/roof/sewer get a bid."** The biggest-dollar items aren't priced inside 4 hours. Promise "triage + priced minor items in 4h, bid-backed ask within 48h," or the free-file promise gets triggered constantly.
3. **"Billed to the sponsoring broker"** while the target is solo agents at other brokerages: their designated broker has to approve and pay, which is a sales step the plan doesn't budget for. Confirm with the attorney whether agent-paid via the broker of record works.
4. **The full disclosure paragraph on every outbound SMS** reads like a bot and burns goodwill. Put the full text on the email and the amendment, and use a short tag on texts. The attorney decides the minimum.
5. **The company holds a TREC brokerage license and keeps other brokers' client files.** The firewall promise should be written into the agent agreement now, not only "if Punchlist Realty launches."

**Verdict:** Yes, it's worth the next 12 months, as long as the founder treats the first 13 weeks as a kill-or-continue bet. Pure per-file SaaS has a modest ceiling, so the rest of the year only pays off if the gate opens a brokerage or institutional-seller path.
