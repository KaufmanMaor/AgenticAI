# PM sign-off on Concept v3 (Option Desk)

**(a) SIGN-OFF, with conditions.** The company is the right one (a B2B tool, a licensed designated broker, one moment in the deal, code holding the limits). But the pilot's volume and two of the gate tests don't add up yet, so fix them before week 2.

**(b) Biggest remaining risk: repeat use and payment at the 9pm moment.** Agents may praise the drafts and still not route their next stressful option period to us. Value can be proven on paper; habit can't.

**Cheapest test (~$0, weeks 2-5, before any live file):** a pre-sale.
- When we recruit them, ask each of the 10 agents to **prepay a 3-file pack at $149/file ($447)**, refundable if we miss the 4-hour turnaround.
- **Pass:** ≥ 6 of 10 prepay. Fewer than 4 → stop before spending the concierge budget.

**(c) Inconsistencies:**
1. **30 files from 10 agents in weeks 4-12 is unlikely.**
   - At ~15 buyer sides a year, an agent has about 1 option period every 3-4 weeks, so 10 agents × ~8 weeks gives roughly **20-25 files**, fewer while recruiting runs to week 5.
   - Fix: recruit **15-18 agents**, or move the gate to week 16.
2. **Gate (c) "≥ 60% pay for a further file" measures deal timing, not willingness.**
   - With 2 free files each and about 3 files per agent in the window, many agents never reach a paid file by week 12.
   - Replace it with the prepay test in (b).
3. **Gate (a) is not defined tightly enough.**
   - Comparing the agent's sealed *ask* with our *outcome* is apples to oranges. Comparing ask to ask rewards bigger asks, which is the nitpicking Ruling 5 rejects.
   - Define the increment as our final concession minus (sealed ask × the agent's historical realization ratio from their past files). Add a guardrail: **no more option-period deaths than the agent's own baseline rate**.
4. **The plan has no concierge expert-time gate.** If the broker co-founder needs more than 2h per file at file 30, this is a services business. Add the criterion "≤ 90 min per file and falling."
5. **The Austin ceiling doesn't fit a $3-4M seed.**
   - $1M ARR at $249 is about 4,000 files a year, roughly 15% of Austin's ~25-30K buyer sides.
   - The 12-month plan must include Dallas, Houston and San Antonio (same TREC forms and the same license) before the seed raise, or the seed story is Texas, not Austin.
6. **The 4-hour guarantee is untested during the free files.** A "file is free" guarantee means nothing when the file is already free. Track the turnaround anyway and report it at the gate.

**Verdict: YES.** The pain is real and paid for (~$10.8K of commission at risk per dead deal), there is a legal path, and the thesis can be killed in 13 weeks for under $1M, which is exactly the bet a founder should take for 12 months, provided the Texas expansion is planned from day 1.
