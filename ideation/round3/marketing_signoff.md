# Marketing sign-off on Concept v3

**(a) SIGN-OFF.** v3 fixes my three Round 2 flaws: one privacy promise, sponsors buy slots rather than data, and the Hooked framing is gone. Nothing here makes the pilot fail.

**(b) Biggest risk: candidates may not opt in.** Every gate depends on employed engineers building a hidden profile before the event, at a brand they don't know. Going from 60 profiles to 25 held intros means about 40% conversion, which is very aggressive.

**Cheapest test: a fake door, run now, before the T-21 gate.**
- A co-branded landing page: "Companies hiring at DevOpsDays: browse privately", with the 5-line privacy text and a "notify me" button.
- Placed in one organizer newsletter slot and the DevOps Israel groups.
- About ₪0 and one week. A/B test two privacy lines.
- At least 4% of recipients sign up: proceed. 1–2%: rethink the candidate funnel.

**(c) Inconsistencies:**
1. The niche is mid-level (3–8 years), but the examples ("12 Senior Backend", "Senior SRE") say "senior". Pick one.
2. 5 companies × ₪2,000 is ₪10K gross, or ₪7.5K after the 25% share. Reaching ₪12–21K needs 6–10 companies or a Hiring Partner deal, and the full-credit guarantee defers revenue. "Break-even" isn't supported.
3. T-21 falls on Dec 1, the last day of Reversim. Move the gate to Nov 27.
4. Blind invites go to profiles before any yes. That fits "nothing leaves the event", but the privacy page must say so in plain words.
