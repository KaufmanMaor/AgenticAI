# Round 3: VP HR / TA sign-off on Concept v3

**(a) SIGN-OFF.** No blocking objection. The coffee code, the 5-field card, blind invites and the founding-price guarantee answer my Round 2 concerns. Condition: the hiring-manager briefing and signed code must exist before the first event.

**(b) Biggest remaining risk: hiring managers don't actually show up.** TA will prepay, then the team lead is pulled into a release or reserve duty on the day. The intro guarantee then becomes refunds, and candidates meet recruiters instead of peers. **Cheapest test:** in the 10 discovery calls, make the prepay ask conditional on a *named* hiring manager accepting a 2-hour calendar invite for Dec 22 within 48 hours. Count accepted invites, not verbal yeses.

**(c) Inconsistencies:**
1. **Revenue does not add up.** 5 companies × ₪2,000 = ₪10K gross and ₪7.5K after the 25% share, below the stated "₪12–21K." The range only works with 6–10 companies or a Hiring Partner sale. Say which.
2. **The guarantee equals the gate.** 25 held intros on the day equals 5 per company × 5 companies. Hitting the gate exactly means any company below average triggers a full credit. Set the gate at ≥35, or define the credit as pro-rata.
3. **Rulings 3 and 4.** With Open mode deferred, companies see no cards in v1. The 5-person rule applies only to counts and invite targeting. Reword it.
4. **Ruling 9.** Candidates don't know their "employer's clients," and the companies registry doesn't list clients. Make it candidate-declared, not automatic.
5. **Rulings 8 and 13.** "Never sees a rejection" conflicts with "companies reply within 48h." Pick one: a neutral "not this time" message within 48h.
