# PM sign-off on Concept v3

**(a) SIGN-OFF, with conditions.** Nothing in v3 makes the plan fail. The gates, the guarantee and the deferrals are the right ones.

**(b) Biggest remaining risk: candidate demand.** Will employed mid-level engineers actually build a profile and knock? Supply can be bought. Senior candidates' trust can't. Everything rests on getting 60 profiles by T-3 and 25 held intros.

**Cheapest test (~₪0, before T-21):** a fake-door test.
- The organizer posts a co-branded "See who's hiring at DevOpsDays. Browse privately" link in its mailing list and in dev WhatsApp and Telegram groups.
- The page lists **only companies that have signed letters of intent**.
- Measure click rate, then how many start a profile, then how many leave a phone number.
- **Pass:** at least 3% of recipients start a profile.

**(c) Inconsistencies:**
1. **Ruling 4 contradicts Knock-only.** A card threshold of k≥5 and per-company viewing caps imply companies browse candidate cards. That's Open mode, which is deferred.
2. **Ruling 3: who picks the 10 invite recipients?** Picking them requires seeing profiles. Make it "10 invites per role, sent automatically to every matching profile."
3. **Economics:**
   - 5–8 companies × ₪2,000, minus the 25% rev share, comes to ₪7.5–12K, not ₪12–21K.
   - A single ₪20–30K Hiring Partner deal would exceed the stated range on its own.
   - Credits from the 5-intro guarantee can push revenue lower still.
4. **Ruling 9 overstates the block list.** The public companies registry doesn't show company groups, and nothing shows "employer's clients." Say "group plus hand-curated list plus self-declared."
5. **Ruling 8 ("never sees a rejection") clashes with the 48h reply pledge.** Silence *is* the rejection. Add a neutral "not this time" status after 48h.
