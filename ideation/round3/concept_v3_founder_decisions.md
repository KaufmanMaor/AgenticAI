# Concept v3: Founder decisions after Round 2

*The founder's rulings on every conflict raised in Round 2. Each ruling cites the role(s) it came from.*

## One-line
**Hallway** (Hebrew: מסדרון) is the hiring layer for professional events. Companies that are hiring are visible. Candidates stay invisible until they knock. Each knock turns into a 15-minute coffee intro with someone who does the job, held at the venue, with a next step booked before either person leaves.

## Rulings on the conflicts

| # | Conflict | Ruling | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deleting everything at 72h conflicts with the 14-day metric and with exporting to the company's hiring system | **One promise: "Nothing leaves the event unless you say yes to a specific company."** Data from unmatched people is deleted 72h after the event, with a deletion receipt. For an accepted intro, a minimal record (who, when, whether a next step was booked) is kept with consent for 90 days. Export to the company's hiring system is a separate consent tap for each company. | Marketing, PM, HR, Tech |
| 2 | The venue QR gate conflicts with matching before the event | **Presence is a status, not the gate.** Candidates can build a profile up to 72h ahead, but it stays hidden. Companies see only totals ("12 Senior Backend people are coming") and only once at least 5 people share that role and seniority. Knocks open once a candidate checks in at the venue, or 24h before for people who hold a ticket. | Tech, PM |
| 3 | Open mode in v1 | **Deferred to v1.1.** The pilot runs on Knock plus a **blind invite-to-knock**: a company can send up to 10 anonymous "we'd love to meet a Senior SRE" invites to matching profiles, and learns who someone is only if that person knocks back. | PM, HR, Tech |
| 4 | Whether candidate cards can be re-identified | No free text. A card appears only if 5 or more people share its role and seniority. Each company has a viewing cap. Agency recruiters are excluded. | Tech |
| 5 | Price | **Founding price ₪2,000 per event** for the first 3 events, with a **guarantee of 5 held intros or full credit** toward the next event. List price later is ₪3,500. Test charging ₪300 per extra intro above 10. Sponsors can buy a **Presenting Hiring Partner** package (₪20–30K): a branded coffee corner, first pick of time slots and a stage mention. **We never sell exclusivity or data.** | BizDev, PM, HR |
| 6 | Who sells it | The organizer sells it to its sponsors **and** to companies that are hiring but don't sponsor. Most dev-conference sponsors are tool vendors, not big hirers. The organizer gets a 25% revenue share plus the Hiring Partner package. | BizDev |
| 7 | Hiring manager on site | Each company must have **at least one team lead or hiring manager for a 2-hour block**. Recruiters can run the rest. Everyone signs and gets a briefing on the Hallway Coffee code of conduct (allowed and forbidden questions). | HR, PM |
| 8 | Leaks outside the database | WhatsApp notifications are neutral ("You have an update from Hallway"), with no company names on the lock screen. Each candidate gets 5 knocks per event and never sees a rejection. | Tech |
| 9 | Blocking the candidate's own employer | Automatic and fails closed. It covers the whole company group, employers the candidate left recently, the employer's clients, and a personal block list. It is built from the public companies registry plus lists we curate by hand. | Tech |
| 10 | Partnership with Hooked | **One learning coffee only**, to learn their real QR scan rates. No partnership. We stop saying "Hooked for jobs" in public now. | Marketing |
| 11 | Niche | Israeli DevOps, backend and AI engineers at mid level (3–8 years), across 3 events: **DevOpsDays TLV (Dec 22, 2026)**, then 2 more in the dev and AI scene. | All |
| 12 | Video booth | Deferred until year 2. Remote companies can join with a phone video call from a quiet-room slot. | All |
| 13 | Building trust | The organizer co-brands Hallway. Candidates see their own employer shown as blocked, live. Named founders, plus 3 respected engineers who vouch for us. A privacy page reviewed by a lawyer. A pledge that companies reply within 48h. | Marketing |

## Pilot gates (DevOpsDays TLV)
- **Go/no-go at T-21 days:** at least 5 prepaid companies and at least 3 relevant roles each.
- **At T-3 days:** at least 60 candidate profiles.
- **On the day:** at least 25 intros actually held, and **0 cases of a candidate exposed to their own employer**.
- **At +14 days:** at least 10 next steps booked, and at least 3 companies prepay for the next event.
- **Kill or pivot after 3 events** if fewer than 50% of companies rebuy, or if the median company has fewer than 5 held intros.

## Revised economics (BizDev)
- **Event 1:** about ₪12–21K in revenue, which is roughly break-even. This event exists to learn.
- **Path:** about $60K in year 1, about $460K in year 2 and about $1.7M in year 3. The upside comes from an annual plan across all events, expanding beyond Israel (WeAreDevelopers, KubeCon EU) and selling it as a white-label module or to an acquirer (Brella, Swapcard, Bizzabo).
